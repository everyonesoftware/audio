Android / Workflow study 002
Listening first. Playlists next.

Right back where you left off.

Open to the full player, not a library. Your playlist, track, position, and shuffle queue stay put.
When the mood changes, choose another playlist and go straight back to the music.

One workflow. One neutral treatment.
These are three moments in the same experience—not competing themes.
Demo state saved only in this browser · No audio or server connection

01 / Continue, don’t start over

Opening restores the full player, paused. Resume keeps the existing queue and position. It does not shuffle your playlist again.

02 / Switch without the detour

Change playlist opens your recent choices first. Tap a row’s shuffle control to start it and return to the full player. Tap its name to inspect instead.

03 / Keep browsing secondary

No listening history? Start with playlists. Albums and the complete song list live behind Browse library, not on the opening screen.
